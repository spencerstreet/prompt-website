How it feels to use

Put the feelers out. Zero chasing after.

  1. Pick what you’re up for: a drink, a run, dinner.
  2. Pick a day: today, tomorrow, this weekend.
  3. Time and place come pre-filled. Choose who sees it: your friends, a list or a group.
  4. Save. Your friends get a nudge, and you hear the moment someone’s in.
Inside the app

From an idea to the real thing.

Catchup is
  • “Anyone up for a drink at 7?”
  • Spontaneous or planned
  • Your friends, not a crowd
  • Out of the app, into the room
Catchup isn’t
  • Endless group-chat back-and-forth
  • A calendar or productivity tool
  • Ticketed events or strangers
  • Followers, endless scrolling or FOMO
Better with friends

Catchup works once your friends are on it.

Get the app, then send your invite link from Settings or your Friends list. Anyone who joins through it is your friend straight away.
