How it feels to use

Ten seconds to float it. Zero chasing after.

  1. 01Tap Plan and pick what you’re doing.
  2. 02Pick a day — today, tomorrow, this weekend.
  3. 03Time, place and who can see it come pre-filled.
  4. 04Save. Friends RSVP, and you see who’s keen.
